Maker terms

Last updated: 24 novembre 2026

The French version of this document is the reference version.

These terms apply to professionals who claim and complete their place's profile in Les Artisans Sincères. They supplement the terms of use.

1. Claiming a profile

A place's manager can claim its profile (or create it if it does not exist) by giving the place's SIRET number and a business contact. The SIRET is checked with the French government's company search API; the team may ask for further proof of management. The manager then proves the qualification of the manager or of the person in charge of production (ice-cream making is a regulated craft in France): a diploma (CAP ice-cream maker, pastry or chocolate-confectionery, BTM, MOF…), 3 years of professional experience (proof of employment, payslips, employer attestations) or craftsman status (attestation from the chamber of trades). The name on the document is compared with the business manager or the person declared in charge of production.

Once the claim and the qualification are accepted, the profile shows "Verified profile", its SIRET number is displayed and the maker joins the Brotherhood.

The qualification document contains personal data: it is stored in a private space, viewed only by the team, then erased 30 days after the decision. Only the result is kept: type of proof, title, approval date, name of the reviewer.

2. The file, criterion by criterion

For each trade criterion (business status, made on site, recipes, workshop, traceability, additives, commitment), the maker fills in a statement and uploads the requested documents. The team reviews each criterion and document, and approves or refuses them with a reason. When all trade criteria are approved, the profile shows "Detailed approach" and an overall rating (half profile, half customers).

3. Confidentiality of documents

Recipes, invoices and food safety plans are strictly confidential: they are stored in a private space, encrypted by the host, readable only by the profile's managers and administrators, and each viewing by an administrator is logged. Only the information intended for each criterion is public (for example the list of additives, photos of the making process, or the origins of ingredients if the maker wishes). Invoice amounts may be hidden. Documents are kept for the life of the profile, then 3 years after it closes.

4. Sincerity

The maker accepts the Sincerity charter ("Commitment" criterion). They guarantee the accuracy of their statements and the authenticity of their documents, and update their profile if their practices change.

5. Withdrawal of indications and exclusion

A false qualification document leads to the withdrawal of management, the loss of "Verified profile", exclusion from the Brotherhood and suspension of the account, by a reasoned decision.

The "Verified profile" and "Detailed approach" indications may be withdrawn, by a reasoned decision, in the event of an inaccurate statement, an inauthentic document, a change of manager, closure, or a breach of the Sincerity charter. A criterion whose content is changed is reviewed again.

6. Replies to reviews

The maker can publish one public reply per review, courteous and factual. They may not rate their own place, request reviews in exchange for anything, or ask for a review to be removed only because it is negative. They can report a false, abusive or off-topic review.

7. Flavours, today's display, photos and posters

The maker can describe their flavours, update today's display, publish photos they hold the rights to, and print a poster with a QR code inviting customers to rate on site, as well as an allergen poster for today's flavours.

Full transparency. Information about flavours (ingredients in decreasing order, origins, star ingredient, bought-in preparations), cones and containers and commitments is declared by the maker, who is responsible for it. It must be accurate, complete and kept up to date, in particular whenever a recipe or supplier changes.

  • Allergens. A flavour's allergens are deduced automatically from the chosen ingredients. The maker can add allergens, never remove them, and must declare any risk of traces. This information does not replace the mandatory information in the shop (EU Regulation 1169/2011), which remains the maker's responsibility.
  • Organic and supporting documents. “Organic” is only shown once the team has approved a supporting document (attestation from the control body, invoices). Information backed by an approved document is marked “Document checked”; other information is marked “Declared by the maker”.
  • Transparency index. It is calculated by the app alone, using public rules that are the same for everyone; the maker cannot change it.
  • Inaccurate information. Customers can report inaccurate information. If the team confirms it, the maker is notified and must correct it without delay. Deliberately false information, in particular about allergens or organic status, may lead to the profile being hidden and to the exclusion provided for in article 5.

8. Neutrality

Claiming and managing a profile is free. No payment, now or in the future, influences a place's rating or ranking.

9. Termination

The maker can leave their profile at any time by writing to contact@equinoxstrategy.fr. The profile stays online, without the indications linked to management.
