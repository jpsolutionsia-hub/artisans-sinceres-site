Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 novembre 2026

The French version of this document is the reference version.

This policy explains which personal data the Les Artisans Sincères app uses, why, for how long, and your rights. We collect as little data as possible: no real name required, no access to your contacts, no advertising, no advertising trackers.

1. Who is responsible for your data?

Jason Poiblaud – JPC Solutions, Avenue Jean Lartigau, 40130 Capbreton, France, SIRET 915 377 717 00029. Contact: contact@equinoxstrategy.fr.

2. What data, for what purpose, on what basis?

Visitors without an account. You can browse the map, profiles and ratings without an account. No data about you is recorded by the app.

Your account (email address, encrypted password, nickname, declared date of birth, date you accepted the terms). It is used to publish reviews, report content and suggest places. The date of birth is only used to check the minimum age of 15; it is never shown. Legal basis: performance of the terms of use (contract).

Your location. It is only requested while you use the app, never in the background, and after an explanation. It is used to show places around you and, when you post a review, to check that you are less than 100 metres from the place. It is never recorded: for each review we only keep the information "rated on site". You can refuse location access and search for a town instead; you will then not be able to rate. Legal basis: your consent, given on your phone and revocable at any time in its settings.

My allergies. If you enter your allergies in “My account”, they are stored only on your phone, in its encrypted storage, and are never sent to our servers: flavours containing them are flagged on the phone itself. We therefore never have access to them. They are not backed up: you can erase them at any time from “My account”, and they disappear if you uninstall the app.

Your reviews (checklist answers, 1-to-5 rating, optional comment and photo, date). They are published with your nickname. Photos are resized and their hidden information (including location) is removed on your phone before sending. Legal basis: contract.

Fighting fake reviews. To keep ratings sincere, we check on-site presence, the delay between two reviews, the absence of links with the rated place and the creation of accounts in series. These checks use the account creation date and review history; we do not keep IP addresses for this. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in publishing reliable ratings, and the legal duty of fair information on reviews (French Consumer Code, article L111-7-2).

Reports and moderation (reason, details, reasoned decision). Legal basis: legal obligations (EU Digital Services Act and French Consumer Code) and legitimate interest.

Maker area (SIRET, business contact, statements, supporting documents: recipes, invoices, food safety plan, photos). The SIRET is checked with the French government's company search API. Confidential documents are stored in a private space, readable only by the maker and administrators. Legal basis: contract (maker terms).

Makers' transparency (flavour ingredients, containers, commitments, and supporting documents: supplier invoices, attestations from the organic control body, technical data sheets). Product information is public; supporting documents are stored in a private space, readable only by the managers of the profile and the team, and only the result of their check (“Document checked”) is shown. Legal basis: contract (maker terms).

Makers' proof of qualification (type of proof, title, name of the qualified person, copy of the diploma or attestation). It is used to check that the maker carries out a regulated activity with the required qualification. The document is stored in a private space, viewed only by the team, and erased 30 days after the decision; only the result is kept. Legal basis: contract (maker terms).

Blocks and word filter. The users you block are recorded with your account to hide their reviews on all your devices. Published texts are compared with a list of forbidden words. Legal basis: contract (terms of use) and legitimate interest (moderation).

Notifications and statistics. Each of these optional uses has its own choice in "My account", off by default and as easy to withdraw as to give. At launch, the app uses no audience measurement tool.

Administration security. Every administrator action is recorded in a log, without copying confidential data. Legal basis: legitimate interest (security, traceability).

3. How long?

  • Account: until you delete it, or 3 years after your last sign-in (your reviews are then anonymised).
  • Reviews: 36 months after publication; they stop counting towards the rating after 24 months.
  • Location: never kept.
  • My allergies: never on our servers; only on your phone, until you erase them.
  • Makers' documents: for the life of the profile, then 3 years after it closes.
  • Transparency supporting documents: for the life of the profile, then 3 years after it closes.
  • Qualification document: 30 days after the decision (only the result is kept afterwards).
  • Reports and decisions: 3 years after the decision.
  • Administration log: 3 years.
  • Host's technical data (connection logs): for the short period set by the host, for security purposes.

4. Who receives your data?

Your data is never sold or used for advertising. It is processed by service providers (processors) bound by contract:

  • Supabase (database, accounts, files, server functions), with storage in the European Union (Union européenne, centre de données Amazon Web Services de Paris (région eu-west-3)). As Supabase is a non-EU company, any access from abroad is governed by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.
  • The email service (account confirmation, password reset, alerts to makers).
  • The map background provider (OpenFreeMap, OpenStreetMap data): to display the map, your phone downloads map images, which technically sends your IP address to this provider, without an account or tracking.
  • The French government's company search API only receives the SIRET number to check.
  • Google or Apple, only if you choose to sign in with them.

5. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and the right to give instructions about your data after your death.

Directly in the app, under My account, you can:

  • view and correct your nickname and date of birth;
  • export all your data (readable file);
  • delete your account, choosing to keep your reviews anonymously or to delete them.

For any other request, write to contact@equinoxstrategy.fr: we answer within one month. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

6. Minimum age

You must be at least 15 to create an account (digital age of consent in France).

7. Security

Encrypted connections, passwords never stored in plain text, access rules checked by the server for every piece of data, private space for makers' documents, mandatory two-factor authentication for administrators, regular backups. In the event of a data breach, the CNIL is notified within 72 hours and, where necessary, the people concerned.

8. Changes

In the event of a significant change, you will be informed in the app before it takes effect.
