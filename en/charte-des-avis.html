Review charter

Last updated: 24 novembre 2026

The French version of this document is the reference version.

This charter describes how reviews are collected, checked, published and sorted, in accordance with article L111-7-2 and articles D111-16 to D111-19 of the French Consumer Code.

Are reviews checked?

Yes. Every review is checked automatically before publication:

  • on-site presence: the phone's location is checked at the time of the vote (less than 100 metres from the place), then discarded;
  • one review per place every 6 months for the same account;
  • no review by the manager or a claimant of the profile on their own place;
  • confirmed account (verified email address) and minimum age of 15;
  • detection of series of reviews from very recent accounts: these reviews are read by the team before publication;
  • word filter: a comment containing insults or hateful language is refused and must be rephrased; a comment containing a serious accusation (fraud, food poisoning…) is read by the team before publication.

After publication, reviews can be reported by anyone and are then examined by the team.

Publication time

A review is published immediately after the automatic check. If it is held for review, it is published or refused within 7 days at most.

Retention

A review counts towards the rating for 24 months, stays visible for 36 months, then is deleted automatically. Its author can delete it at any time.

Sorting

Reviews are shown from newest to oldest, with no other criterion. The date of each review is shown.

Incentives

Reviews are neither paid for nor requested in exchange for anything by the publisher. Makers are forbidden to offer anything in exchange for a review.

Reasons for refusal or removal

A review may be refused or removed if it is:

  • false or misleading (no real experience of the place);
  • abusive, defamatory or discriminatory;
  • off-topic;
  • unlawful;
  • written with a conflict of interest (competitor, relative, employee…).

Each user can also block another user: they no longer see that user's reviews (reversible in "My account").

The author of a refused or removed review is notified in the app, with the reason. A review is never removed because it is negative. The maker can publish a reply to each review.

How the rating is calculated

The full method, in plain words, is one click away from every rating ("How is the rating calculated?"). In short: the 7-question checklist counts for 75% and the 1-to-5 rating for 25%; old reviews and reviews from recent or suspicious accounts weigh less; while there are few reviews, the rating is brought closer to the average; it is only shown from 5 reviews. No rating can be changed by hand and no payment influences a rating or the ranking.
