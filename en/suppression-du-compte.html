Delete your account and data

Last updated: 24 novembre 2026

The French version of this document is the reference version.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Les Artisans Sincères and tap My account.
  2. Tap Delete my account.
  3. Choose:

- Keep my reviews, without my name: your reviews stay published with no link to you;

- Delete my reviews too: your reviews disappear and ratings are recalculated.

  1. Type the requested word to confirm.

What is deleted

  • your account (email address, password), your profile (nickname, date of birth, privacy choices);
  • your notifications and review photos;
  • your profile claims (for makers).

What may be kept

  • your reviews, anonymised, if you chose to keep them;
  • documents uploaded for a place's profile, which concern the business and are kept up to 3 years after the profile closes;
  • administration log entries, without your data, for 3 years.

Without the app

Write to contact@equinoxstrategy.fr from your account's email address asking for its deletion. We answer within one month.

Before deleting your account, you can also export your data (My account → Export my data).
