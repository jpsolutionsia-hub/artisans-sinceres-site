Terms of use

Last updated: 24 novembre 2026

The French version of this document is the reference version.

1. Purpose

The Les Artisans Sincères app, published by Jason Poiblaud – JPC Solutions, helps the public recognise genuine food artisans, starting with ice-cream makers, through a community rating given on site and the profiles that makers have checked. These terms govern its use. The app is free.

2. Access and account

Browsing the map, profiles and ratings is open to all. An account is needed to rate, report content or suggest a place. You must be at least 15. You undertake to give accurate information, keep your password confidential and use only one account. Your nickname must not be misleading or abusive, nor use the name of a place.

3. Review rules

A review must reflect your own, real experience of the place. It is given on site (less than 100 metres, checked by the app) and the same account can only rate a place once every 6 months. The following are forbidden:

  • reviewing a place you manage, work at, or with which you have a conflict of interest (competitor, relative, employee…);
  • reviews written in exchange for something (gift, discount, payment) or at the request of a third party;
  • abusive, defamatory or discriminatory content, or content infringing privacy;
  • off-topic, promotional or unlawful content;
  • photos of recognisable people without their consent.

4. Moderation and reporting

An automatic filter refuses insults and hateful language and has serious accusations checked before publication. Anyone can block a user to stop seeing their reviews, and report a review, reply, photo or profile with the "Report" button, or by writing to contact@equinoxstrategy.fr. Reports are reviewed by the team; the decision is explained and sent to the person who reported and, where applicable, to the author of the content. Manifestly unlawful content is removed promptly. A review is never removed because it is negative. Detailed rules are in the Review charter.

5. Your content

You remain the owner of your reviews and photos. You grant the publisher, free of charge and for as long as they are kept, the right to reproduce and display them in the app and its presentation materials. You guarantee that you hold the rights to the photos you send.

6. Ratings and profiles

Ratings are calculated automatically according to the method published in the app. Nobody, including the publisher, can change them by hand, and no payment influences them. The indications "Verified profile" and "Detailed approach" describe information provided by the maker and reviewed by the team; they are not an official guarantee.

7. Suspension and deletion

If these terms are breached, the publisher may remove content, reduce the weight of an account's reviews or suspend an account, by a reasoned decision. You can delete your account at any time in "My account".

8. Liability

The publisher provides the app with care but without any guarantee of uninterrupted service. Reviews express the opinion of their authors, who are responsible for them. Profile information is provided by the makers. The publisher acts as host of user content and promptly removes it once aware that it is manifestly unlawful.
