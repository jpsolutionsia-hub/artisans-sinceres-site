Sincerity charter

The French version of this document is the reference version.

By ticking the "Commitment" criterion, the maker undertakes, for their place, to:

  1. Make on site the products presented as home-made, in their own workshop.
  2. Work from raw ingredients, without presenting ready-to-use industrial bases or mixes as artisanal production.
  3. Honestly declare the stabilisers, flavourings and colourings used, and keep this list up to date.
  4. Provide authentic documents and never present an altered or borrowed document.
  5. Inform the team of any change in practice, main supplier, manager or address.
  6. Respect customers: never rate their own place, never request reviews in exchange for anything, reply to reviews courteously.
  7. Accept checks by the team, including, later on, a visit.

Failure to comply with this charter may lead to the withdrawal of the "Verified profile" and "Detailed approach" indications, by a reasoned decision.
